FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. You tell us about your business, we do the rest. If you can fill in a form, you can get a website.

How long does it take?

Once we have your details, logo and photos, we'll usually send you a preview within a week.

What do I need to give you?

Just your business details, the services you offer, and your logo and a few photos if you have them. We sort out everything else, including your domain name and Google listing. If you don't have a logo or photos, we'll work with what you've got.

What counts as a "content update"?

Changing text, swapping photos, updating prices or opening hours, adding a review or making a small layout tweak. Brand new pages or features are quoted separately, and we'll always tell you the price before doing anything.

Will I show up on Google Maps?

Yes, that's included. We set up your Google Business Profile (the listing that shows on Google Maps and in local search) and keep it up to date for you. Google needs to confirm you're a real business, so you'll do a quick one-off verification, usually by video or phone. After that, we look after it. Asking happy customers to leave Google reviews is the best thing you can do to climb the rankings.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain name is included in your plan, and we register it in your business's name, so your web address is always yours. If you ever leave, we'll transfer the domain to you and hand over your website files.

Can I cancel?

On the Standard plan, cancel any time with 30 days' notice. The Nothing upfront plan has a 12-month minimum term, then it runs month to month.
